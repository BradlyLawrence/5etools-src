Save character drafts, homebrew feats and workshop designs before exporting. Packages include saved data only. Characters include their private notes, inventory and current resources; sheet presets and crafting designs can be shared on their own.

Export

Save Draft keeps buffs, debuffs and display settings inside the character. To share that setup separately, use Download layout at the bottom of the combat sheet. Uploaded layouts also appear here as presets.

Import

Review the entries below before importing. Identical entries are skipped. Conflicts keep your existing copy unless you explicitly choose Replace. Required homebrew feat definitions are automatically included when exporting characters or feats. Import those dependencies along with the entries that need them. Item designs become workshop recipes, not owned equipment.
