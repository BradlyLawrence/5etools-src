Homebrew feats

Characters · Packages

Create private feat definitions, then take them through the character's normal feat choices. Prerequisites and automated modifiers use the existing rules engine. Manual effects remain table-resolved.

Prerequisites

Checked at the level the feat is taken. Leave empty for no prerequisites.

Effects

Use numeric modifiers for supported statistics; use Manual for mechanics the sheet cannot resolve. Matching bonus types follow normal stacking rules. Toggle effects get a Combat checkbox.
